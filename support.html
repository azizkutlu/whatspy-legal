Frequently asked

  • What is WhatSpy? WhatSpy is an entertainment app that uses AI to generate playful insights (tone, sentiment, interest signals, patterns) about chat conversations you choose to import. Results are AI-generated estimates and are for fun only.
  • Does WhatSpy read anyone's messages? No. WhatSpy does not access any account, device, or private messages. It only analyzes conversation text that you provide.
  • How do I manage or cancel a subscription? Subscriptions are billed through your Apple ID. Open the Settings app → tap your name → Subscriptions, then manage or cancel WhatSpy there.
  • How do I request data deletion? Email us at the address above and we'll help with your request.
